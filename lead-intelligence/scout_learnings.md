## Strategic Positioning & Delivery Boundaries

### Hard CMS & Delivery Filter (The "No WordPress Maintenance" Rule)
* **Preferred:** Modern / custom stack where Context & Muse builds cleanly in modern web technologies.
* **Acceptable (WordPress / Legacy CMS):** **ONLY** when the engagement is sold as a **completely standalone conversion layer** deployed on a dedicated subdomain (e.g. `book.gopaschal.com` or `quote.company.com`) or as a clean iframe/modal embed. The client's existing WordPress site remains completely untouched.
* **Automatic Disqualifier / Reject:** Any client or RFP that requires direct WordPress theme surgery, Elementor hacking, PHP maintenance, plugin wrangling, or ongoing legacy CMS webmaster support.

---

## The Standalone Conversion Layer Pitch
When pitching high-value established companies (like Paschal):
* **Never pitch:** *"We will redesign your WordPress pages."*
* **Always pitch:** *"We deploy a dedicated, high-converting intake engine on `book.company.com` that connects to your existing site with a single button link. Your existing site stays untouched, and you get a zero-friction qualification & routing machine."*
