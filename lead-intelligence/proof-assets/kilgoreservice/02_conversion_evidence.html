Evidence-Backed Hypothesis Ledger (CM Series)

CM-KIL-0101 Acquisition Routing HIGH PRIORITY

Primary conversion paths rely on generalized top-level CTAs without intent-matched landing states.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Header and hero present general 'Schedule Service' or 'Contact' actions.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Commercial buyers, urgent replacement shoppers, and routine repair inquiries enter identical triage paths, creating drop-off for higher-ticket commercial requests.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Deploy intent-matched modular landing funnels with distinct fast-tracks for Commercial Facilities vs Residential Emergency vs System Replacement.
CM-KIL-0102 Service Discovery MEDIUM PRIORITY

Broad multi-trade catalog requires visitors to self-diagnose technical categories prior to conversion.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Website exposes multiple trade/service lines across standard menus without a problem-first guided selector.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Visitors experiencing ambiguous issues face friction navigating menu hierarchies rather than intuitive symptom-first triage.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Implement an interactive 'What do you need help with?' guided symptom-to-service selector on high-intent entry pages.
CM-KIL-0103 Qualification & Intake HIGH PRIORITY

Lead intake utilizes static multi-field forms without progressive disclosure or dynamic qualification.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Intake forms present standard text fields with mandatory phone collection before value delivery.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Static forms with high upfront friction depress initial submission rates, particularly on mobile devices.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Transition to a 2-step conditional micro-step intake with instant trade pre-qualification.
CM-KIL-0104 Booking & Conversion Velocity HIGH PRIORITY

Inquiry workflow requires post-submission phone callback rather than confirmed appointment scheduling.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Intake collects customer details as a contact request with an implied callback window.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Quote and inquiry decay accelerates while customers await manual dispatch contact; emergency buyers call competitors.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Integrate direct real-time dispatch calendar booking with dynamic emergency queue prioritization.
CM-KIL-0105 Geographic Routing MEDIUM PRIORITY

Multi-market operational footprint lacks automated ZIP-level customer routing at point of entry.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Regional facility hubs are listed in directories, but entry CTAs do not pre-qualify service area eligibility upfront.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Dispatches from out-of-territory inquiries require manual administrative filtering and lead redirection.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Implement instant ZIP code validation and dynamic nearest-hub routing on all primary conversion entry points.
CM-KIL-0106 Confirmation & Lead Recovery MEDIUM PRIORITY

Post-action reassurance and abandoned intake recovery mechanisms are not publicly observable.

Ground Truth Evidence:
Intake forms do not display pre-submission response time SLAs or partial session persistence.
Commercial Impact Hypothesis:
Uncertainty around response time suppresses submission rates; users who abandon mid-form are lost without automated recovery.
Recommended Context & Muse Implementation:
Add transparent arrival/callback SLA badges and automated 2-way SMS confirmation loops.
